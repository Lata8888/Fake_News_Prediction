# Fake_News_Prediction (Machine Learning)
In this project, we employ a Logistic Regression model for the binary classification of news articles into two categories: Real or Fake. The Logistic Regression algorithm is well-suited for this task due to its simplicity and effectiveness in binary classification problems. The model outputs a binary value, where '0' indicates that the news is real, and '1' signifies that the news is fake. This approach enables a systematic and data-driven method to evaluate the credibility of news articles.
